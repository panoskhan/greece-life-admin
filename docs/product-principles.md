# Greece Life Admin — Product Principles

## Mission
Make everyday administration in Greece easier to understand and easier to start.

## Product rule
A person should be able to describe a situation in plain Greek and get a clear next step, an appropriate checklist, and a link to the authoritative source.

## Trust rules
- Never pretend to be a government service.
- Prefer official Greek sources for procedures, requirements, deadlines, emergency information and contact details.
- Show when a source was last reviewed when practical.
- Never invent a phone number, office, requirement, fee, deadline or legal/medical claim.
- Personal checklists and saved items remain local to the user's device.
- Do not require an account for core functionality.
- Medical, medicine and legal sections provide navigation/information, not diagnosis, prescriptions or personalised legal representation.

## People we serve
- Greek residents
- Greeks abroad
- EU citizens living in Greece
- Third-country nationals and newcomers
- Older people and people with accessibility needs
- Small businesses and self-employed people
- Anyone who does not know which public authority handles a problem

## Core experience
1. Tell us what is happening.
2. Pick or refine the situation.
3. Get a short explanation of what to do next.
4. See the official authority/source.
5. Follow a simple checklist.
6. Save progress locally.
7. Report outdated or broken information.

## Future priority
The app should become a trusted navigation layer over Greece's fragmented public-service ecosystem, not another unofficial database that attempts to replace authoritative services.
